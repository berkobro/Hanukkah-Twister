What it is

A star that turns into a tower

The Hanukkah Twister is a hand-sized fidget toy shaped like a six-point star. It's made of a solid center and six star-shaped rings, all nested inside each other. Every ring is twisted like a screw, so whenever the rings slide, they spin.

1

Push the center up

Press the middle from underneath and the rings spin and corkscrew out, one after another, into a spiraling cone.

2

Let gravity do it

Hold the outer ring and let go of the rest. The rings whirl downward on their own and hang in a spinning cone — like a dreidel that never falls over.

3

Press it flat

Push the rings back in and they twist home, lying flat as a Star of David again. Then do it all over. And over. (Eight nights is just the start.)
